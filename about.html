About the Artist

Megan Klein

Owner & tattoo artist, Velvet Vessel Ink — St. George, Utah

Biography

A Studio Built On Intention

I never wanted Velvet Vessel Ink to feel like a shop you walk into off the street. I wanted it to feel like a room you're invited into — quiet, considered, entirely about the work in front of us.

Tattooing found me the way most meaningful things do: sideways. I spent years drawing before I ever picked up a machine, filling sketchbooks with botanical studies and portrait work long before I understood that could be a career. When I finally apprenticed, everything clicked into place — the discipline of drawing met the permanence of skin, and I haven't looked at either the same way since.

What started as a fine-line side practice slowly grew into a full studio built around one idea: every tattoo should feel intentional. Not rushed off a flash wall, not chosen because it was easy to book — chosen because it means something to the person wearing it.

The Journey

From Sketchbook to Studio

My path into tattooing was slow and deliberate — years of formal art study, an apprenticeship that humbled me more than once, and a long stretch of guest spots before I ever had a chair of my own.

Opening Velvet Vessel Ink in St. George was about creating the studio I always wanted to be tattooed in myself: unhurried, private, and genuinely collaborative. I take a limited number of clients so that every piece — from the first sketch to the final healed photo — gets my full attention.

Philosophy

"A tattoo should say something true about the person wearing it — even if only they know what that is. My job isn't to talk you into something trendy. It's to help you find the version of your idea that you'll still love in twenty years."

— Megan

The Studio

St. George, Utah

Velvet Vessel Ink is a private, appointment-only studio in St. George — set against the red rock landscape of southern Utah. The exact studio address is shared directly with clients once a request is approved, keeping sessions calm, private, and by appointment only.

Every session is one-on-one. No walk-in flash wall, no rushing between chairs — just you, the design, and the time it takes to get it right.
