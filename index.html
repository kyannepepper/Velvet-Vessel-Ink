The Artist

Meet Megan Klein

Velvet Vessel Ink is a private studio built around slow, considered tattooing — where every piece starts as a conversation, not a walk-in flash sheet.

Based in St. George, Utah, Megan works primarily in fine line, floral, realism, black & gray, and custom lettering, taking on a limited number of pieces so each one gets the attention it deserves.

How It Works

The Tattoo Process

  1. 1

    Discover & Browse

    Explore Megan's completed portfolio or the inspiration gallery to find a starting point — or come with your own fully-formed idea.

  2. 2

    Submit a Request

    Share your idea, placement, size, and any reference images, then choose an available date from the studio calendar.

  3. 3

    Personal Review

    Every request is reviewed personally by Megan — nothing is booked automatically until she confirms the details and approves a date for your piece.

  4. 4

    Your Session

    Once approved, you'll receive appointment details and personal prep & aftercare guidance ahead of your visit to the studio.
