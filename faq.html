Everything clients ask most before their first session. Don't see your question? Reach out directly.

Pricing & Deposits

How much will my tattoo cost?

Pricing depends on size, detail, and placement. As a general guide: small pieces run roughly $100–$200, medium pieces $200–$400, and larger pieces $400–$700. Use the calculator on the booking page for an instant estimate — final pricing is always confirmed by Megan after reviewing your specific idea.

Do you require a deposit?

No — Megan does not currently require a deposit to submit or hold a request. Just submit your idea and preferred date, and she'll follow up to confirm details.

Booking & Scheduling

How does the booking process work?

Browse the portfolio or inspiration gallery (or bring your own idea), choose an available date on the calendar, and submit a request. Megan personally reviews every request before it's approved — nothing is booked automatically.

How far in advance should I book?

It varies by season, but a few weeks out is a safe bet for most placements. Larger, multi-session pieces should be requested further in advance.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Velvet Vessel Ink is an appointment-only studio — no walk-ins. This lets Megan give every client's design her full attention before the appointment even begins.

Can I reschedule my appointment?

Life happens — just reach out as soon as you know you need to move your date, and Megan will work with you to find a new available day. Plenty of advance notice makes rescheduling much easier for everyone.

Getting Tattooed

Is there an age requirement?

Yes — clients must be 18 or older with valid photo ID. No exceptions are made, even with parental consent.

Do you do cover-ups?

Yes. Cover-ups take extra planning since the design has to work around existing ink, so please include clear, well-lit photos of the current tattoo when you submit your request.

Can you create a fully custom design?

Absolutely — most of Megan's work is custom. Share references, ideas, or a combination of designs from the inspiration gallery, and she'll develop original artwork based on your request ahead of your appointment.

How do I prepare for my appointment?

Get a good night's sleep, eat beforehand, stay hydrated, and avoid alcohol for 24 hours prior. Full details are on the aftercare & preparation page.

What's the healing process like?

Most tattoos surface-heal within 2–3 weeks, with deeper healing continuing for a couple of months. Full guidance — including what to avoid — is on the aftercare page. Megan's personal instructions given at your appointment always take priority.
